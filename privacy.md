# Privacy Policy — Sudoku Zig

Sudoku Zig is a classic Sudoku puzzle game. This policy describes what data the app handles.

## Data collection

Sudoku Zig does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

## Data storage

All game data — your puzzles, progress, bookmarks, and settings — is stored locally on your device. It never leaves your device.

## Network access

The app does not send any data over the internet. The only network activity is a local connection to itself (loopback) used to render the app's interface; no data is transmitted to any external server.

## Permissions

The app requests the internet permission solely for that local connection. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files without your explicit action.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the GitHub repository: https://github.com/mwigzell/sudoku-zig
